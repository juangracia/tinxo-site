Privacy Policy for TINXO

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

1. Introduction

TINXO ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile party game application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We do not collect any personally identifiable information. TINXO is designed to work completely offline and does not require accounts, registration, or any personal data.

2.2 Game Data

All game data (player names, game settings, preferences) is stored locally on your device using IndexedDB. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted to any servers
  • Is not accessible to us or any third parties
  • Remains on your device until you delete the app or clear app data

2.3 Anonymous Analytics

We use PostHog for anonymous usage analytics to improve the app. This includes:

  • What we collect: App opens, game selections, button clicks, app crashes
  • What we DON'T collect: Player names, game content, personal information, device identifiers
  • Purpose: Understanding which features are popular and identifying bugs
  • Data retention: 90 days
  • Third party: PostHog (https://posthog.com/privacy)

2.4 In-App Purchases (Premium Version)

In-App Purchases are not currently available but may be offered in future versions. If offered, purchases would be processed through Apple App Store / Google Play Store via RevenueCat:

  • Payment processing: Handled entirely by Apple App Store / Google Play Store
  • What RevenueCat would collect: Purchase receipt, device type, transaction ID
  • What we would see: Anonymous purchase status (premium unlocked: yes/no)
  • We would not see: Your name, email, payment method, or any personal information
  • Third party: RevenueCat (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

3. How We Use Information

The limited anonymous data we collect is used solely to:

  • Improve app performance and stability
  • Understand which games are most popular
  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Make decisions about future features

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties, except:

  • PostHog: Anonymous analytics data (as described above)
  • RevenueCat: Purchase verification data (as described above)
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights

5. Children's Privacy

TINXO is rated 4+ on the App Store and includes a Family Mode for age-appropriate content. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us immediately.

6. Data Security

Since all game data is stored locally on your device and we collect minimal anonymous analytics, security risks are minimal. We use industry-standard practices for the limited data we do collect.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data: All game data is stored locally on your device in the app's storage
  • Delete your data: Delete the app or clear app data in device settings
  • Opt out of analytics: Currently not available, but analytics are anonymous and minimal
  • Request information: Contact us about our data practices

8. International Users

TINXO is available worldwide. Anonymous analytics data may be processed in the European Union where PostHog servers are located. By using the app, you consent to this data transfer.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by:

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Posting a notice in the app (for major changes)

Your continued use of TINXO after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Third-Party Services

TINXO uses the following third-party services:

  • PostHog: Anonymous analytics - Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: In-app purchase management - Privacy Policy
  • Sentry: Crash and error reports (stack traces, app version, device type), no personal information - Privacy Policy
