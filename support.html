Help with TINXO, the party game.

Questions, feedback, or a bug to report? Email juanmgracia@gmail.com and we will get back to you. For a bug, tell us your device model, iOS or Android version, and what you were doing when it happened.

What is TINXO?

TINXO is a set of seven party games for a group on one phone: Truth or Dare, Most Likely To..., Never Have I Ever, The Impostor, Forbidden Word, Who's Who, and TINXO Roulette. You add the players, pick a game, and pass the phone around.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add, edit, or remove players?

On the home screen, type a name and tap Add. Tap the pencil next to a name to edit it, or the ✕ to remove it. Drag a player to change the order. Colors are assigned automatically.

How many players do I need?

At least 2. Each game has its own limits, between 2 and 12 players. The game list shows the range for each game.

How do I start a game?

Add your players, tap Choose Game, and pick one of the seven games.

How do I end a game early?

Tap Menu at the bottom of the game screen.

What do Family Mode and the intensity levels do?

Open Settings on the home screen. Family Mode shows only kid-friendly content. Intensity has two levels: Mild (easy questions and challenges) and Normal (a bit more daring).

How do I change the language?

TINXO uses your device language when it can: Spanish (Argentina, Mexico, Spain) or English (US, UK). Otherwise it starts in Spanish (Argentina). To change it, open Settings on the home screen and tap a language.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. All the content is inside the app, and there are no accounts.

Does TINXO cost anything?

No. All seven games are free, and there are no in-app purchases at this time.

Sounds are not playing

Check that Sounds is on in Settings, that your phone is not on silent, and that the volume is up.

My players or settings disappeared

TINXO stores everything on your device. Deleting the app, clearing its data, or tapping Reset Everything in Settings removes your players and settings.

The app crashed or is slow

Close the app fully and open it again. Make sure you have the latest version from the store. If it keeps happening, email us with the details above.

I have an idea for TINXO

Tap Suggestions? on the home screen, or email us. We read every message.

What data does TINXO collect?

Your players and settings stay on your device. TINXO sends anonymous usage analytics and crash reports, with no names or game content. See the Privacy Policy for details.
