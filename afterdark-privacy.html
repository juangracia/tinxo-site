TINXO After Dark

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

TINXO After Dark is developed by Juan Gracia. It is a party game for adults (18+). The short version: there are no accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email or location. Player names stay on your device. The only things that leave your device are anonymous usage analytics and, if you choose to use it, the short theme you type to make custom cards. Nothing is sold or used for advertising.

Information stored on your device

  • The player names you add, your settings and your unlock.
  • If you turn on Crews, a short history of recent nights (who played and which games), kept for 30 days. It is off by default and you can clear it in Settings.
  • Your answer to the 18+ question.

This data stays in the app's storage on your device. It is not uploaded to us or shared with anyone. Deleting the app removes it.

Anonymous usage analytics

To see which games people play and where the app can improve, TINXO After Dark sends usage events to PostHog, on servers in the European Union. Events say things like: which game started, how many players, how many cards, which screen showed the unlock, and whether a purchase went through. Each event includes the app version and platform.

  • Events are linked only to a random ID that the app creates on your device. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or advertising ID.
  • Events never include player names, card text or the themes you type.
  • Automatic click tracking and session recording are turned off.

AI custom themes

Custom themes are optional. When you type a theme (for example, "our trip to Bariloche") and tap Make cards, the app first asks you to agree. If you agree, the theme text and your language are sent to our server, which passes them to a third-party AI service (Anthropic) that writes the cards. The AI service returns the cards and does not receive your player names, your device identifiers or anything else from the app.

  • To keep the service available and safe from abuse, the server checks that the request comes from the genuine app using Apple's App Attest. That check uses a random key made on your device, which is not linked to you.
  • The server keeps daily request counters under a one-way hash of your key and network address, for about two days. It keeps generated cards for a theme for up to one week, so the same theme does not need a second request.
  • Type themes about your group, not about private details of real people. Themes about minors or illegal acts are refused.
  • The service is not offered in mainland China, Hong Kong and Macau.

Purchases

The unlock is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your payment details. The app only learns, from Apple, whether you own the unlock.

Network data

When the app sends analytics or a theme request, the receiving service sees your device's IP address as part of the connection. We do not use it to identify you. The card games work with no internet connection at all.

Information we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Accounts or sign-in data.
  • Player names or card text.
  • Advertising data. We show no ads and do not track you across other apps and websites.

Age

TINXO After Dark is for adults aged 18 and over. The app asks for confirmation on first launch and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Your choices

  • Do not use custom themes: nothing is sent to the server.
  • Delete the data on your device by deleting the app.
  • To ask about data linked to your device or to request deletion, email us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
