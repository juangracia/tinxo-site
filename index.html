TINXO

The party game you pass around

Seven group games on one phone. Add the players, pick a game, and pass the phone.

The games

  • Truth or Dare, the classic.
  • Most Likely To...: vote on who is most likely to do something.
  • Never Have I Ever: find out what everyone has done.
  • The Impostor: find the player who does not know the secret word.
  • Forbidden Word: avoid your word and make the others say theirs.
  • Who's Who: guess who wrote each answer.
  • TINXO Roulette: quick random challenges.

How it works

  • Plays fully offline. All the content is inside the app.
  • No accounts and no sign-up.
  • Family Mode and two intensity levels, Mild and Normal.
  • In Spanish (Argentina, Mexico, Spain) and English (US, UK).
